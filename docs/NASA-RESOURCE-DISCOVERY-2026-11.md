## Catalog findings

- **SVS 5688** compares tropical Pacific sea surface temperature anomalies for 2025 and 2026 using NASA JPL MUR SST and a seven-day running average. The official legend-bearing 1670 × 835 JPEG is the first candidate for a local audition. [NASA SVS 5688](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5688/)
- **SVS 5687** compares ocean apparent visible wavelength for corresponding months of 2025 and 2026. Its PACE association and distinctive colors make it a useful companion visual. Interpretation requires the distinction between an ocean color indicator and phytoplankton abundance. [NASA SVS 5687](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5687/)
- Both entries carry NASA catalog tags for Earth Information Center, EIC display and Hyperwall. Their daily filenames can serve new bytes over time. A test needs a fixed download, digest and visible dates.
