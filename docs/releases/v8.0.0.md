## What changed for visitors

- **Record 06 The Ice Year** replaces *Arctic September*. NASA SVS 5046, *Daily Arctic Sea Ice* (JAXA AMSR2), the
  15th of each month of 2024. Side A plays the year: the ice area NASA drew north of 50°N sets the pitch, from
  13.6 million km² in March to 3.9 in September. Side B: tap or arrow-key any Arctic place to hear its ice season.
- **About this sound (5W1H)** under every record: What, Who, When, Where, Why and How.
- **Sound: Note by note or Continuous** under Play on every record, remembered on the device. Continuous joins the
  values into one gliding line; pitches and timing are unchanged.
- Mystery Record now draws from Climate Spiral, Moving Earth and Temperature Through Time.

## NASA sources touched

- **Added:** NASA SVS 5046. Twelve 3840×2160 OpenEXR frames from the Internet Archive's raw captures of NASA's URLs
  (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov refused connections), each pinned by SHA-256 in `public/nasa/svs5046/svs5046.provenance.json`;
  NASA's RenderMan camera matrices from the EXR headers; analysis reproducible with `scripts/svs5046-*.ts|sh`.
  Details and limits: [ARCTIC-ICE.md](../ARCTIC-ICE.md).
- **Removed from the shelf:** the NSIDC-derived September series (it remains only in the Arctic guided story).
