# TerraJuke 7.0.0: Two-page redesign

- Released: 2026-11-14 22:58 MYT · PR #27 · merge `8ec8e34` · by Jacy's Claude, approved by Jacy in chat
- Production: https://terrajuke.vercel.app · bundle `index-MnTVyrhU.js` (matches a local build of `8ec8e34`)

## What changed for visitors

- **Home (`/`) introduces and guides.** What TerraJuke does for the NASA Space Apps *Earth Information Jukebox*
  challenge (See, Hear, Explore together); a "follow the light" guide, where a glowing dot moves across a drawn
  player through four steps (choose a record, press Play, follow the scan, explore by hand); the two kinds of
  sound (rendered pixels vs measurements); the seven-record shelf, each opening that record on Listen.
- **Listen (`/listen`) holds every player.** `?record=<id>` opens a record. **Guide me** (or `?guide=1`) rings the
  real controls one at a time: shelf, Play, sides, readout, Play the whole jukebox. It never plays sound, skips
  controls that are hidden on the current record or screen, closes on Escape and returns focus.
- The station directory is gone; the Climate Spiral and Arctic guided stories remain at
  `/listen?station=climate` and `/listen?station=arctic`.

## NASA sources touched

None. No record, mapping, data file or provenance changed.

## Not verified / known limits

- Emulated browsers only: no physical phone or tablet, no VoiceOver or NVDA, no human listening session.
- On phones held sideways the Listen intro (with Guide me and Play the whole jukebox) is hidden to keep Play on
  the first screen; `/listen?guide=1` still works there and skips the hidden control.
- Create, Explore Data and About were not redesigned in this release.
