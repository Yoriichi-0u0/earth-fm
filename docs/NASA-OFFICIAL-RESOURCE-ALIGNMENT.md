## New public evidence and a candidate worth testing

The live [SVS 5688 JPEG](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a005600/a005688/elnino_eic_ssta_globes_wLegend_1670x835_latest.jpg) changed at the same URL. The November 12 retrieval is a **1670 × 835 JPEG, 473,761 bytes**, SHA-256 `ecafa8bcfffc29205799da027eaf732783e2eeb3ce1676136c6b844735a5e7a0`. Its visible dates are November 11, 2025 and November 11, 2026. The Pacific Lab original shows November 9 in both years and has SHA-256 `f6a7fad2dcf92096e8f7d8393f8ae97c9821a563aeb5834861c0c5a83fee4634`. Both credit NASA SVS and Kel Elkins. Keep the approved A/B input frozen. A routine refresh adds no new interaction and would require a new pixel and audio comparison.

NASA's *Signs of El Niño in 2026* offers a stronger research alternative if a map comparison is called for. The catalog has a **1920 × 1080 unlabelled SST anomaly JPEG** (`elnino_2026_ssta_1010_nolabels.02200_preview.jpg`, catalog 713.4 KB), separate dated overlays and a legend, plus 1080p and 2160p MP4s. Its companion views show PACE OCI apparent visible wavelength and altimetry-derived sea surface height anomaly. Those variables carry different units and answer different questions. A flat map pair could support a more meaningful east–west comparison if matching extents and projection are established. Continue to label the resulting notes as pixel sound. The catalog establishes these formats and the published article establishes the science context. The alternative files were not decoded or auditioned in this checkpoint. [NASA SVS 5683 downloads and credits](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5683/) · [NASA Science explanation](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/nasa-satellites-observe-historic-2026-el-nino/)

Terrasonic’s public repository pointed to **SVS 5176**. NASA credits the visualization to NASA SVS and Kel Elkins and identifies the Pacific-centered frame set as equirectangular and derived from JPL MUR SST. The catalog links the `MUR-JPL-L4-GLOB-v4.1` product. Two October 10 frames, one from each year, are available in the same published sequence. Both URLs returned HTTP 200 to `HEAD`. The first 64 KiB of each returned HTTP 206. The EXR headers have identical 4096 × 2048 data and display windows, pixel aspect, screen window and camera transforms. This supports common rendering geometry. Geographic bounds, longitude origin, exact observation time, anomaly climatology and pixel registration across the full rasters still need verification. The EXRs are rendered images and require a documented offline conversion before browser sampling. ## Recommendation

Lead with **Climate Spiral A/B** in the research demo. On Side A, let a visitor move the scan and hear a NASA visual respond immediately. On Side B, let them hear selected GISTEMP observations and read their values. The two sources support one climate story while making the origin of each sound inspectable. Pacific Lab is the best reserve if the published resources favor a dated comparison, or if a source makes the same-position comparison scientifically clearer. Moving Earth is useful if a supplied animation communicates something a still cannot. Adding more standalone records offers less value than making one verified pairing easy to understand and use with a keyboard or screen reader.

At publication, collect every entry as displayed, including its title, URL, description and explicit instructions. Follow official collection pages to exact downloads and data products. Record format, version, bytes, SHA-256, timestamps, units, credits and restrictions as applicable. Treat a catalog or dataset match separately from a byte-for-byte match. Check the official list for instructions about visual and numerical pairing before deciding which option earns an experiment.

If an official asset earns a probe, use one small still and the current transport in isolation. Show the original dates, legend and credit. Compare audible variation with Spiral and verify silent inspection, explicit playback, switching and cancellation. Test keyboard access and narrow viewports. Add a separate measurement path only for verified observations with known units, coverage and reference. [Adapter readiness and test checklist](NASA-RESOURCE-READINESS.md)
